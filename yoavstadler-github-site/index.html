Introduction

Hi, I’m Yoav Stadler, a game designer who thinks in systems first.

At AppCentral, I shipped 8 mobile titles as Product Manager, a role that meant owning level design, balancing and player retention, not just the product roadmap.

These days, Howest Digital Arts & Entertainment is where I’m sharpening that craft, building out my coding, 3D art and design fundamentals.

My design process starts with how players actually behave. The systems come after, not the other way around.

Today might be the chance to let your talent bloom.
Maybe tomorrow, the day after, or next year...
