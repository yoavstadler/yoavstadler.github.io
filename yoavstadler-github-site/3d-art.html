The Risen

Animation preview

An RTS building of an artificial sun built for the Solthari - a light-worshipping faction whose elemental magic depends on sunlight, and whose power is fading as an encroaching cloud layer cuts off their mountain peak from the sun. The structure represents an engineered replacement light source, built to sustain their fading civilization. Designed to fit the assignment's faction visual brief (Art Nouveau and Greek-inspired architecture, pastel greens/yellows/aquamarines, gold and crystal materials), modeled and animated in Blender, textured in Substance Painter, and assembled, lit, and rendered in Unity - including building and upgrade animations across three growth stages.

Artisan's Desk

A low-poly prop set built from a randomized assignment prompt - profession: noble jeweler. Framed as a workspace for a jeweler in service of the Medici family, crafting the ornate, poisoned jewelry the family was rumored to use against political rivals. Modeled in Blender, textured in Substance Painter. Fully rotatable model above.

Sukuna's Malevolent Shrine
