GAME JAM LOG - BIG RED, LITTLE RED

Wave Spawning Difficulty

An iteration log tracking a broken difficulty curve during the jam - early waves that punished new players and late waves that had gone slack, plus the real numbers behind fixing both.

  • Moving from a linear difficulty ramp to a sine-based curve to give players breathing room after spikes
  • Retuning enemy-type introductions so new enemies teach their behavior before punishing mistakes
  • The actual tuning values: Big Red's dash duration, Little Red's grenade cooldown, and per-wave enemy counts
  • What playtesting revealed - including a compounding-upgrade problem that wasn't fully solved by the jam's end
