What I did

  • Designed the core systems
  • Balanced each player's upgrade pool

Clock Out

Team

A top-down stealth puzzle game. You're sneaking out of the office early using the one tool on your desk nobody suspects: an old rotary phone. Dial a desk, make it ring, and watch coworkers walk over to answer it, clearing your path. Made with tijmenDC and bronzenkid.

What I did

  • Designed the desk-calling mechanic
  • Designed and balanced the Manager, who calls every 30 seconds to micromanage and comes for you if you don't answer
  • Designed the phone-hand, used to answer the Manager and hang up a wrong dial
  • Designed and balanced the level layout
