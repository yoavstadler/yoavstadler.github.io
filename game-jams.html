Big Red, Little Red

Type
Game jam
Role
Systems Designer, Programmer & 3D Artist
Team
4
Engine
Unity
Duration
2 days

A 2-player local co-op action game where two tomatoes battle an army of vegetables together, combining dash, shooting, and grenade abilities to survive increasingly difficult waves. Made in 2 days with three friends.

Between rounds, each player picks from their own separate upgrade pool, so the two characters specialize into distinct playstyles over a run. The core design focus was local co-op feel, responsive controls, and a wave-based difficulty curve - the Systems Design Log shows how that curve was iterated on.

What I did

  • Designed the game's core systems and mechanics
  • Balanced each player's separate upgrade pool
  • Designed, balanced and programmed the enemies and their wave-difficulty curve (documented in the Systems Design Log)
  • Programmed Little Red, the second player character, end to end

Clock Out

Type
University game jam
Role
Game & Systems Designer
Team
3
Engine
Unity
Duration
1.5 days

A top-down stealth puzzle game about sneaking out of the office early using the one tool on your desk nobody suspects - an old rotary phone. Dial a desk, make it ring, and watch coworkers walk over to answer it, clearing your path. Made with tijmenDC and bronzenkid.

What I did

  • Designed the desk-calling mechanic: dial a desk on the rotary phone to pull coworkers away from your escape route
  • Designed and balanced the Manager, who calls to micromanage you every 30 seconds and comes running if you don't answer
  • Designed the phone-hand mechanic, used both to answer the Manager's calls and to clear a mistaken dial
  • Designed and balanced the level layout
